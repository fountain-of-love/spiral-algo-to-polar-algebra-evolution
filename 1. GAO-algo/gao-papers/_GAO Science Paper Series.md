## 🧭 Introduction

This companion series focuses exclusively on **Geometry-Aware Optimization (GAO)** as a living algorithmic lineage. Each paper explores one **FIBO stage** of GAO’s internal spiral — showing how a single algorithm evolves from local geometric heuristics into self-adaptive, resonant intelligence. GAO thus becomes not only a method for solving TSP-like problems but a **microcosm of algorithmic evolution itself**.

Each stage captures a qualitative leap in GAO’s awareness: from deterministic geometry (0–1a) through dynamic adaptation (2–3), expression and communication (5), governance and meta-learning (8), and ultimately unity and re-seeding (13). The GAO spiral reveals that even within a single algorithm, the full Fibonacci pattern of systemic growth is present.
