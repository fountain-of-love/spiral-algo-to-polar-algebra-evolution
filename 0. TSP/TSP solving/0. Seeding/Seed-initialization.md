At **Stage 0**, randomness isn’t neutrality — it’s **entropy without awareness**.

Greedy and local heuristics are **path-dependent systems**: their final coherence depends on the initial seed. A smarter seed doesn’t just start earlier — it **starts aligned**, lowering systemic disorder from the very first move.

## ⚙️ Practical Seeding Archetypes

Each method reflects a different **field logic** of $\text{Origo}$ — how potential is distributed before evolution.

| Archetype | Essence | Why it helps | FIBO/Blueprint Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spatial clustering seeds** | Start from centroids or medoids ($\text{k-means, Voronoi}$) | Preserves local neighborhoods for coherent greedy growth | Aligns with $\text{Adaptatio}$ (flow continuity) even before Stage 2 |
| **Poisson-disk / blue-noise seeding** | Ensures even angular/radial spacing | Balances coverage; prevents early over-clustering | $\text{Dualitas}$: balances density gradients |
| **$\text{MST}$ / $\text{1-tree}$ inspired seeds** | Order points along a minimal spanning skeleton | Encodes global topology into the seed | $\text{Trinitas}$: container of latent topology |
| **Angle-radius stratified seeding** | Distribute seeds evenly in ($\text{r}, \theta$) bins | Matches $\text{GAO}$’s rotational coherence | Pre-activates Stage 3 $\text{Synchronia}$ patterns |
| **Learning-guided seeds** | Lightweight model predicts promising starting nodes | Learns instance statistics; minimizes expected refinement effort | $\text{Expressio}$: information feedback loop at seed level |
| **Ensemble seeding** | Generate many diversified seeds, run quick greedy passes, keep top $\text{k}$ | Explores entropy space cheaply and parallelly | Mirrors $\text{Circulatio}$: multi-seed exploration as distributed intelligence |
