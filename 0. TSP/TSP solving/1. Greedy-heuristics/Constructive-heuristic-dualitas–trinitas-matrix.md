## 🧩 2. Structural Insight

This framework reveals that the constructive heuristic space is primarily latent:

**Two Existing Complete Pairs:**
* **Cost** ($\text{NN} \leftrightarrow \text{CI}$)
* **Angle** ($\text{Sweep} \leftrightarrow \text{GAO}$)

**Five Latent Pairs Ripe for Discovery:**
* **Density** ($\text{Density Greedy} \leftrightarrow \text{DAO}$)
* **Proportion** ($\text{Ratio Greedy} \leftrightarrow \text{PAO}$)
* **Symmetry** ($\text{Mirror Greedy} \leftrightarrow \text{SAO}$)
* **Topology** ($\text{Adjacency Greedy} \leftrightarrow \text{TAO}$)
* **Rhythm** ($\text{Alternating Greedy} \leftrightarrow \text{RHO}$)

These latent pairs represent the **unoccupied cells** of the constructive-heuristic landscape, each introducing a new mediating field variable while remaining within the low-complexity, greedy paradigm.
