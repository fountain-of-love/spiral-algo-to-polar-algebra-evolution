# 🌀 Stage 1b – Trinitas / Containment Field

---

## 🔹 Essence

**Stage 1b** marks the **formation of a stable reference frame**.

After the wild pull of Dualitas, the system seeks **orientation** — a **field** or **womb** that holds polarity in relationship.

In constructive TSP heuristics, this manifests as introducing a **spatial coordinate system** (polar or ordered) that contains local decisions.

It is no longer “pick the closest anywhere,” but **“sweep within an ordered frame.”**

---

## 🧩 1. Representative Algorithms

| Family / Algorithm | Core Mechanism | Behavioural Signature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sweep / Angular Sort** | Sort points by polar angle around a chosen origin or centroid; connect sequentially. | Produces **smooth, planar tours**; very fast; brittle to irregular densities. |
| **Radial Sort / Outward–Inward Spiral** | Sort by radius (or angle $\to$ radius); move from center outward or vice-versa. | Creates spiral patterns; good for circular or layered data. |
| **Space-Filling Curve Mapping (Hilbert / Morton)** | Map coordinates onto a 1-D ordering via a locality-preserving curve. | Deterministic; respects proximity; orientation-sensitive. |
| **Polar Grid Binning** | Divide space into angular bins; traverse bins in order. | Adds discrete containment; basis for GAO-type algorithms. |
| **Convex-Hull Follow** | Start with hull points; sweep along boundary before interior insertion. | Natural geometric envelope; smooth perimeter flow. |

* **Complexity:** $O(n \log n)$ (sorting dominates).
* **Performance:** better than raw NN on structured data; 5–15% over optimum typical; weak under irregular distributions.

### ✅ Stage 1b Summary

**Stage 1b – Trinitas = The Law of Containment and Field**

* **“From contrast arises orientation.”**
* **Purpose:** stabilize polarity by embedding it in a geometric frame.
* **Representative Algorithms:** Sweep, Radial Sort, Space-Filling Curves, Convex-Hull follow.
* **Structure:** sorted field $\to$ sequential traversal; polarity held in geometry.
* **Energetic Pattern:** balanced containment; proto-rhythmic order.
* **Outcome:** planar, rotation-dependent tours — fast, clean, but brittle under density variance.
* **Transition Vector:** moves toward Stage 2 (Adaptatio) when the frame starts adjusting dynamically to spatial texture.
