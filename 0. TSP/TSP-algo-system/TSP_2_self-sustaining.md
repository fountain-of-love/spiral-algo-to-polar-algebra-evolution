# 🌍 Stage 2 – The Life-Support System ($\text{Adaptatio}$ + $\text{Textura}$)

---

## 🔹 Essence

**Stage 2** is where the system first becomes **self-sustaining** (homeostatic).

The reactive, unstructured decisions of earlier stages (0 – 1b) evolve into **careful maintenance** ($\text{Adaptatio}$) and **organized stability** ($\text{Textura}$). It's no longer just *choosing* or *ordering* — it's **maintaining integrity while growing**.

In algorithmic terms, Stage 2 is the realm of **adaptive insertion** and **modular pre-structuring** — the step from "just greed" to "**constructive intelligence**." Stage 2 is the **crucible**: the first self-organizing intelligence in constructive computation, where geometry becomes alive enough to care for its own stability.

| Aspect | $\text{Adaptatio}$ (Flow) | $\text{Textura}$ (Fabric) | Systemic Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Function** | Regulates local additions to preserve coherence | Builds durable scaffolds and modular order | Maintains smooth, scalable growth |
| **Timescale** | Short-term (per insertion) | Medium-term (emerging pattern) | Multiscale stabilization |
| **Representation** | Tour as living line (feedback loop) | Tour as structured lattice (support) | Dynamic morphology |
| **Computation** | Re-evaluates incremental cost | Organizes space for future insertions | Efficient self-organization |

**Core interaction:** **$\text{Adaptatio}$ *feels*** $\to$ **$\text{Textura}$ *remembers***. $\text{Adaptatio}$ smooths local tension; $\text{Textura}$ embeds those corrections into geometry. Each insertion updates the fabric; each fabric pattern constrains new insertions.
