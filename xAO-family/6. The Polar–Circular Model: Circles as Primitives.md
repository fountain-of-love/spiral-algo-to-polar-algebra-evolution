# ⚙️ The Polar–Circular Model: Circles as Primitive Operators

*Building directly on the previous section, where we reframed geometry as a polar–harmonic field,  
we now formalize the computational model that treats circles, rotations, and radii as primitive operators.  
This defines the **Polar Primitive Calculus (PPC)**, a foundation for the Polar Continuity Class (PCC)  
and for the native geometric expression of GAO and related xAO algorithms.*

---

## 3.1 Polar Primitive Calculus (PPC)

We adopt a **unit-cost RAM model** whose primitive objects are points \( p \in \mathbb{R}^2 \)  
and **primitive operators over circles**:

| Operator | Definition | Description |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Circle** | \( C(c, r) \) with center \( c \) and radius \( r > 0 \). | Fundamental geometric entity. |
| **Rotation** | \( \text{Rot}(p; c, \phi) \) — rotate point \( p \) by angle \( \phi \) about center \( c \). | Primitive rotational transformation. |
| **Incidence** | \( \text{On}(p, C) \) — predicate: point \( p \) lies on circle \( C \). | Membership test. |
| **Intersection** | \( C(c_1, r_1) \cap C(c_2, r_2) \to \{ p^-, p^+ \} \) | Produces 0, 1, or 2 intersection points. |
| **Equal-radius / Concentric predicates** | \( r_1 = r_2 \), \( c_1 = c_2 \). | Equality or concentricity test. |
| **Arc measure** | \( \text{Arc}(C; p \to q) \in [0, 2\pi) \) — oriented arc from \( p \) to \( q \). | Primitive angular measurement. |
| **Radial order** | For fixed \( c \): \( \text{rad}(p \mid c) = \|p - c\| \), \( \text{ang}(p \mid c) \in [0, 2\pi) \). | Primitive polar coordinates; comparisons are unit-cost. |

### Derived Constructs

Lines, segments, angles, and Euclidean distances become **derived** via circle relations:

- **Line through \( p, q \)** — limiting chord or tangent construction.  
- **Segment length** — chord formula on a suitable circle.  
- **Angle between segments** — difference of arc measures.  
- **Distance \( d(p, q) \)** — either Euclidean norm or \( 2R \sin(\Delta\theta/2) \) when evaluated on \( C(c, R) \) with \( p, q \in C \).

This swaps the **Cartesian basis** (lines, segments, orientation tests) for a **polar–circular basis**  
where *rotation and radius are first-class* and linear objects emerge secondarily.

## 3.2 Problem Setting (Unchanged Objective, New Primitives)

Given a finite set \( P \subset \mathbb{R}^2 \)  
and a cost functional

\[
L(\pi) = \sum_i \| \pi_{i+1} - \pi_i \|,
\]

we study **constructive heuristics** whose selection and state are expressed entirely in **PPC primitives**.

---

## 4. Polar Continuity Class (PCC) — Repositioned

Under the **PPC** model, PCC is no longer a “nice subset” of Euclidean instances;  
it is the **native continuity class** whose structure is defined by circular primitives.

### Definition 2 (PCC(κ, ρ, σ) — Polar Continuity Class)

Fix a reference center \( C \) (e.g., centroid of \( P \)).  
An instance \( P \) belongs to \( \text{PCC}(\kappa, \rho, \sigma) \)  
if there exists a Hamiltonian cycle \( \pi \) such that, writing  
\( \theta_i = \text{ang}(\pi_i \mid C) \), \( r_i = \text{rad}(\pi_i \mid C) \),  
the following **bounded-variation conditions** hold for all \( i \):

1. **Rotational boundedness (curvature):**  
   \[
   |\theta_{i+1} - \theta_i| \le \kappa, \quad \text{wraparound on } [0, 2\pi)
   \]

2. **Radial smoothness:**  
   \[
   |r_{i+1} - r_i| \le \rho, \quad \text{number of radial extrema} \le \sigma
   \]

3. **Non-degenerate spacing:**  
   For some \( \delta > 0 \),
   \[
   \min_{i \neq j} |\theta_i - \theta_j| \ge \delta
   \]
   preventing angular aliasing.

**Intuition:**  
The optimal tour has **bounded turn**, **limited radial oscillation**, and **no angular clustering**  
that could disrupt continuity-aware decisions.

## 5. GAO in PPC — Native Formulation

Let \( C \) be fixed. Maintain state  
\[
\Theta_t = (\theta_t, r_t), \quad \theta_t = \text{ang(current} \mid C)
\]

GAO selects the next city via a **continuity-augmented objective** expressed entirely in PPC primitives:

\[
v_{t+1} = \arg_{v \in P \setminus S_t} \min \Big(
d(\text{current}, v)
+ \lambda_\theta |\text{ang}(v \mid C) - \theta_t|
+ \lambda_r |\text{rad}(v \mid C) - r_t|
\Big)
\]

with angular gating constraint  
\[
|\text{ang}(v \mid C) - \theta_t| \le \Delta\Theta.
\]

All terms are primitive; distances may use chord length or Euclidean norm without altering semantics.

---

## 6. Main Result (Outline): Exactness on Harmony-Bounded PCC

**Theorem 1 (GAO optimality on strongly harmony-bounded PCC)**

There exist bounds \( (\kappa^*, \rho^*, \sigma^*, \delta^*) \) and weights \( (\lambda_\theta, \lambda_r, \Delta\Theta) \)  
such that for any instance \( P \in \text{PCC}(\kappa, \rho, \sigma) \) with  
\[
\kappa \le \kappa^*, \quad \rho \le \rho^*, \quad \sigma \le \sigma^*, \quad \delta \ge \delta^*,
\]
the GAO tour equals the optimal Euclidean TSP tour.

**Complexity:**  
\( O(n^2) \) naïve or \( O(n \log n + nB) \) with angular bins and spatial indexing.

---

### Proof Sketch

1. **Polar neighborhood lemma.**  
   Under \( \kappa \le \kappa^* \), the optimal successor lies within an angular sector \( O(\kappa) \)  
   and radial window \( O(\rho) \) around the current state.  
   Edges leaving this window create turns or jumps dominated by local two-opt replacements  
   constructed via circle–circle intersections.

2. **Local dominance lemma.**  
   With tuned \( \lambda_\theta, \lambda_r \), any move breaking continuity is strictly dominated  
   by an admissible one of no greater length.

3. **Global stitching.**  
   Repetition along the cycle yields a tour composed solely of admissible edges.  
   By uniqueness under \( (\delta^*, \sigma^*) \), this tour coincides with OPT.

4. **Complexity bounds.**  
   Angular gating restricts candidates to \( O(n/B) \) on average;  
   nearest evaluation per step is \( O(n/B) \) or \( O(\log n) \) with indexing.

---

### 6.1 Approximation in the Weak Harmony Regime

If \( \kappa \le \bar{\kappa} \), \( \rho \le \bar{\rho} \) (weaker bounds),  
GAO achieves a \((1+\varepsilon)\)-approximation on \( \text{PCC}(\bar{\kappa}, \bar{\rho}, \sigma) \) for suitable λ’s, with  

\[
\varepsilon = O(\bar{\kappa}^2 + \bar{\rho}/R),
\]
where \( R \) is the characteristic radius.

**Sketch:** Bound detours from continuity violations using local two-opt arc swaps; accumulate via PPC arc measures.

## 7. Discussion — What Changes When Circles Are Primitive

- **Foundational stance:**  
  PCC is not a Euclidean subset; it’s the *native geometry*.  
  Lines and angles emerge from circle–circle relations; GAO’s rules are first-order laws.

- **Algorithm design:**  
  Continuity predicates (bins in \( \theta \), windows in \( r \)) become constant-time.  
  Arc/chord exchanges support matroid-style exchange proofs in polar space.

- **Complexity:**  
  NP-hardness of general TSP remains, but **exactness on PCC** is a **structural** result —  
  like planar or metric special cases in other domains.

> The value lies in isolating a crisp, geometrically defined class  
> where a continuity-aware greedy heuristic is **provably optimal**.

## 8. Minimal Formal Interface (for Implementation)

**State update:**
\[
\Theta_{t+1} \leftarrow (\text{ang}(v_{t+1}\mid C), \text{rad}(v_{t+1}\mid C))
\]

**Gate constraints:**
\[
|\text{ang}(v \mid C) - \theta_t| \le \Delta\Theta, \quad
|\text{rad}(v \mid C) - r_t| \le \Delta R
\]

**Scoring function:**
\[
J(v) = d(\text{current}, v) + \lambda_\theta |\Delta\theta| + \lambda_r |\Delta r|
\]

**Data layout:**  
Angular bins over \([0, 2\pi)\); optional annular buckets for radius \( r \).
