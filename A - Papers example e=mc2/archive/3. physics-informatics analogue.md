Observations
Metcalfe’s Law is the clearest analogue: it literally encodes n² resonance.
Moore’s Law is exponential, not quadratic, but functionally resonates the same way: recursive feedback doubling.
Amdahl/Shannon show limits to resonance — boundaries where the “squared effect” cannot fully unfold.
ML scaling laws are the modern active analogue: improvements follow predictable exponents (not square, but resonance multipliers still).
