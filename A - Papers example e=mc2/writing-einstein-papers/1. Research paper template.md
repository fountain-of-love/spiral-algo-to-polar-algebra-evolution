# Template for a Scientific Paper (0–13 Spiral)

## Abstract  
*(Condensed Stage 0–5 arc)*  
Briefly state the paradox/problem, the principle introduced, the framework, the integration, and the core results. One paragraph.

---

## 1. Introduction *(Stage-0: Void / Latency)*  
Present the central paradox or puzzling observation.  
Highlight why it matters (fertile anomaly, not trivial noise).  
Invite the reader into the tension.

---

## 2. Problem Framing *(Stage-1a: Origination)*  
State the two conflicting principles or frameworks.  
Articulate their incompatibility clearly.  
Emphasize why resolution is necessary.

---

## 3. Guiding Principle *(Stage-1b: Replication)*  
Introduce the new postulate or unifying principle.  
Show how it contains both poles without suppressing either.  
Indicate its general scope, hinting at replication beyond the immediate problem.

---

## 4. Framework and Definitions *(Stage-2: Equilibration)*  
Lay down operational definitions (e.g. simultaneity, reference frames).  
Keep assumptions minimal and balanced.  
Show how the framework avoids contradictions.

---

## 5. Development of the Argument *(Stage-3: Integration)*  

### 5.1 Conceptual Scenarios *(Sensorium)*  
Use thought experiments or vivid setups to anchor abstractions.

### 5.2 Mathematical Derivation *(Conformatio)*  
Present equations step by step, interleaved with prose.

### 5.3 Emerging Principles *(Synchronia)*  
Surface invariants, symmetries, or lawful patterns revealed.

---

## 6. Results and Resolution *(Stage-5: Orchestration)*  

### 6.1 Core Results *(Expressio)*  
State key formulas, relations, or findings concisely.

### 6.2 Applications and Predictions *(Circulatio)*  
Show testable consequences, connections to known experiments, or future applications.

### 6.3 Resolution of Contradictions *(Restitutio)*  
Explicitly show how the initial conflict has been resolved.

---

## 7. Community and Culture *(Stage-8: Amplification)*  
Translate results into teachable form (diagrams, summary bullets, plain-language restatement).  
Suggest further research directions or experiments others can pursue.  
Signal the cultural/epistemic values the principle embodies (clarity, elegance, parsimony).  
Write with an eye toward adoption, pedagogy, and transmission.

---

## 8. Horizon of Integration *(Stage-13: Unification)*  
Indicate how the principle points toward broader unification across domains.  
Name the world-picture or paradigm it hints at.  
Suggest cross-disciplinary implications (other sciences, philosophy, technology).  
Offer meta-principles (the method itself as transferable): how paradox → principle → framework → law.

---

## 9. Conclusion  
Restate paradox, principle, results, and their implications.  
Emphasize both the immediate resolution (stage-5) and the broader horizon (stages-8 and 13).

---
