**The Resonant Capacity Law: A Systemic Refinement of Einstein's Relativity Formula through the Blueprint × FIBO Framework**

---

## Abstract

Einstein's formula, *E = m c²*, defines energy as mass multiplied by the square of the speed of light. Beyond its physical interpretation, this relationship can be reinterpreted as a systemic law describing how transformation capacity arises in any organized system. By viewing *c²* not as a fixed constant but as a resonance multiplier—a feedback-amplified interaction rate—we extend the formula into a general principle: **Capacity (C) = Substrate (m) × (Flow × Resonance)².** This paper situates the reformulation within the Blueprint × FIBO systemic framework, showing how the original relativistic relation maps onto universal patterns of productivity, vitality, and computation across physics, biology, economics, and AI systems. The result is the articulation of a new transdisciplinary law: the *Resonant Capacity Law* (RCL).

**Keywords:** Resonance, Systems Theory, Blueprint Model, FIBO Stages, Einstein, Relativity, Productivity, Feedback, Scaling Laws, Transdisciplinary Physics.
