## 3. Structural Refinement

The square term indicates **recursive propagation**. Squaring in physics often marks *feedback-amplified resonance*—a system interacting with itself through cycles or harmonics.

Examples:

* **Kinetic energy:** ( E_k = ½ m v² ) → velocity feedback through motion.
* **Electromagnetic energy:** ( E ∝ A² ) → amplitude resonance in wave cavities.
* **Hooke’s law:** ( E = ½ k x² ) → harmonic resonance through elastic displacement.
* **Electric field energy:** ( u = ½ ε E² ) → field self-coupling through space.

Thus, the “²” in each case signifies not mere mathematical scaling, but *resonance intensity*.

In systemic language:

> The resonance multiplier (R²) measures the feedback density of the system—how strongly flows amplify themselves through coherence, trust, or algorithmic recursion.
