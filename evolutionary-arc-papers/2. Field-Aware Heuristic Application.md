# 🌐 FIBO Stage 2 – Adaptatio–Textura: Structural Fabric

## **Paper 3: Field-Aware Application — Comparative Flows of NN, CI, SW, and GAO**

### **Abstract**

Having defined the constructive heuristic field, **Paper 3** turns from theory to embodied application. It examines how algorithms behave within varying **spatial fabrics** — noise, scale, and density — revealing how adaptability and structural coherence emerge as performance drivers beyond raw distance. Through extensive empirical testing, we map the *flow signatures* of Nearest Neighbor, Cheapest Insertion, Sweep, and GAO across structured and random distributions.

We introduce the concept of **algorithmic texture**: the visible patterns of flow, overlap, and continuity that arise from repeated application under shifting spatial contexts. By interpreting results as *field responses*, not isolated outcomes, this paper translates benchmarking into an ecological experiment — algorithms adapting to the geometry of their environments.

Results show that GAO exhibits high structural resilience under distortion, CI maintains optimal density control, SW adapts best to clustered fields, and NN remains sensitive but brittle. Together, these insights generate a new typology of algorithmic fabrics.

This stage, **Adaptatio–Textura**, grounds the xAO lineage in living structure: where geometry, environment, and computation weave into a dynamic fabric of adaptive flow — the soil from which ecological intelligence (Stage 3) will arise.
