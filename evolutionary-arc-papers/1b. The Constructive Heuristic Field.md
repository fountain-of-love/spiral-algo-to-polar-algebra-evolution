# 🔺 FIBO Stage 1b – Trinitas: Containment / Field Formation

## **Paper 2: The Constructive Heuristic Field**

### **Abstract**

The evolution from dual metrics to a coherent *field logic* marks a fundamental threshold in algorithmic awareness. **Paper 2** introduces the **Constructive Heuristic Field**, a unified geometric space where classical heuristics — Nearest Neighbor (NN), Cheapest Insertion (CI), Sweep (SW), and Geometry-Aware Optimization (GAO) — are treated as *points within a multi-dimensional continuum* rather than isolated methods.

By constructing an **algorithmic topology**, we reveal how each heuristic embodies a distinct balance of locality, coherence, and adaptability. Through this lens, NN and CI emerge as polar archetypes (cost vs. structure), with SW and GAO mediating as bridging forms. This trinitarian containment transforms algorithmic diversity into a structured ecosystem, enabling hybridization and emergent synthesis.

Mathematically, the paper formalizes this field as a 3D manifold defined by cost gradient, angular continuity, and density adaptation. Empirically, we map each algorithm’s trajectory through this field under varying spatial conditions, showing their interrelations as continuous transformations rather than discrete categories.

This stage, **Trinitas**, establishes containment: the heuristic family becomes a *living field of relations* — the first stable womb of the xAO lineage from which new algorithmic forms will emerge.
