## 🧭 Introduction

This research series traces the **evolutionary arc of constructive heuristics** for combinatorial optimization — from the classical greedy era to the emerging **xAO (Field-Aware Optimization)** framework.

Where traditional heuristics (*Nearest Neighbor*, *Cheapest Insertion*, *Sweep*) operate on local metrics, and GAO introduced geometry-aware coherence, the **xAO lineage** generalizes these methods into **field-based, multidimensional, and resonant systems**.

Each paper represents a **Fibonacci stage** in the evolution of algorithmic awareness, moving from local decision rules to global field integration — eventually expressing a new paradigm of **living algorithms**.
