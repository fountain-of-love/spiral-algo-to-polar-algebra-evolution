Empirically, we reveal that resonant coherence often correlates with improved global optimality and reduced computational turbulence.

This **Expression & Circulation** stage externalizes the xAO identity: the field’s internal intelligence becomes measurable, communicable, and transmissible — allowing algorithms to be compared not only by what they achieve, but by *how harmoniously they evolve*.
