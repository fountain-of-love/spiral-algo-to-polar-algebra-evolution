# 🧭 FIBO Stage 0 – Origo: Ignition of Possibility

## **Paper 0: The Geometry-Aware Spark (GAO Classic)**

### **Abstract**

At the dawn of constructive heuristics, algorithms such as Nearest Neighbor and Sweep operated as purely local greed engines — fast, deterministic, but geometrically blind. This paper introduces **Geometry-Aware Optimization (GAO)** as the *ignition point* in the evolutionary lineage toward field-aware algorithms. GAO re-centers heuristic construction on **angular coherence**: the preservation of rotational continuity around a dynamic centroid. By integrating directionality and spatial symmetry into cost selection, GAO achieves tours comparable to insertion heuristics at near-greedy cost.

We formalize GAO as a minimal geometric extension of NN, derive its computational complexity, and benchmark it on Euclidean TSP instances from TSPLIB. Results reveal a consistent improvement in route smoothness, path coherence, and adaptive scaling with negligible overhead. Beyond performance, GAO represents the **birth of algorithmic awareness** — the first spark of geometry as an active organizing principle within greedy logic.

In the Fibonacci arc, GAO occupies the **Origo**: the origin of possibility from which the xAO lineage unfolds. Each subsequent stage will expand this spark — from local geometry to multidimensional fields, from greedy logic to resonant intelligence.
